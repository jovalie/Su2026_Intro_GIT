# Story Outline

### 1. Main Character Introduction

This is the story about Cadie, a marshmallow that is outgoing and happy to share experiences with others. It hopes to achieve the ultimate sweetness!

### 2. Setting the World

Establish the story's setting and introduce details that make the world feel distinctive, whether it is realistic, futuristic, magical, or unfamiliar.

### 3. The Initial Discovery

Cadie encounters something unexpected and begins investigating what it means.

### 4. Conflict or Challenge

A problem emerges that prevents Cadie from simply returning to normal. They must make a difficult choice or overcome an obstacle.

### 5. Dialogue and Relationships

Cadie speaks with another important character. Their conversation reveals information, establishes their relationship, or creates disagreement.

### 6. Internal Thoughts and Emotions

Show Cadie's private thoughts and feelings as they process what has happened and question what they should do next.

### 7. Technology or Invention

Introduce a fictional device, invention, discovery, or unusual mechanism that becomes important to the story.

### 8. Escalation

The situation becomes more complicated. The characters face greater risks, new obstacles, or consequences from their earlier choices.

### 9. Plot Twist

Cadie actually has an evil twin sibling. They start fighting.

### 10. Major Decision

Cadie must make an important decision based on everything they have learned. Their choice determines what happens next.

### 11. Climax

The air in the chamber was thick and electric, smelling of ozone and ancient dust—a suffocating cocktail that pressed against Cadie’s lungs. This was it: the culmination of every desperate choice, every whispered fear, and every sacrificial moment. Before them stood the Obsidian Engine, not merely a machine, but the physical manifestation of the city's decay, humming with malevolent power. Its central core pulsed with a sickening emerald light that seemed to draw all warmth from the surrounding stone. The main obstacle wasn’t just its brute force; it was the sheer impossibility of defeating something designed to be eternal. Cadie felt the weight of every life they had sworn to protect settle onto their shoulders, pressing them down until their knees trembled. Every instinct screamed retreat, but years of defiance and a single-minded purpose propelled them forward, forcing them into the blinding glow of inevitable confrontation.
With a guttural cry that tore at their throat, Cadie launched themselves across the fractured floor, dodging bolts of pure energy that hissed past where they had stood seconds before. The Engine’s defense systems activated instantly, surrounding them with pulsating barriers and arcane traps—a symphony of lethal geometry. It was not enough to merely destroy it; they had to find its keystone, the single point of vulnerability hidden beneath layers of impenetrable might. Drawing upon a reserve of power they didn't know they possessed, Cadie focused their will, channeling every ounce of conviction into their outstretched hands. The ensuing clash was deafening—a physical blow that reverberated through bone and spirit. Metal screamed against magic; energy fields buckled under the impact of pure, desperate force. They ignored the searing pain in their arms, ignoring the way the world seemed to tilt on its axis, driven only by the absolute necessity of victory.
But the moment the Engine shuddered, its emerald core dimming to a sickly sputter, Cadie did not feel triumph; they felt a profound, aching emptiness. The immediate threat was neutralized, the destructive humming silenced into a deep, echoing silence. Yet, the true consequence washed over them in a devastating wave. Their exhaustion hit like a physical blow, stealing their breath and making their vision swim with black spots. More acutely, they saw the scorch marks on their allies’ faces, the visible toll taken by the sheer brutality of the battle. The cost was not measured in rubble or broken machinery; it was etched into Cadie's own body—a deep, burning ache that signaled a profound depletion of spirit and strength. They sank to one knee, not from defeat, but from the crushing weight of having lived through something so devastatingly absolute. Victory had come at the expense of their very self, leaving them stranded in the quiet aftermath, vulnerable and painfully alive.

### 12. Resolution or Cliffhanger

Either resolve the main conflict and show what happens afterward, **or** end with an unanswered question, revelation, or new threat that leaves the story open for what comes next.
